# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free and constructive community
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or level of
technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing evidence directly.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not the person.
- Protect private information and never post real customer or business data.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety and release readiness.
- Correct mistakes openly and help others participate successfully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, deliberate
disruption, doxxing and publication of another person's private information are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the maintainer contact route described
in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments or
issues and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to
protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public spaces.
